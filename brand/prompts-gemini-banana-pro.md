## 🔧 Variation Tricks

Setelah dapat 1 versi yang Anda suka, gunakan ini di prompt yang sama:

1. **Geser warna dominan ke earth lain**:
   - Default mustard dominant → "Replace mustard accent with clay (#A4795F) tones for a warmer earth feel"
   - Atau slate-dominant → "Slate (#5C6970) accent instead of mustard for a cooler, more serious mood"

2. **Tambah unsur Indonesia subtle**:
   - "Subtle hint of Indonesian batik kawung or parang geometric pattern in
     the topographic background corner, very minimal, integrated naturally
     into the contour-line texture."

3. **Mood shift**:
   - Lebih warm: "Slight golden-hour warmth across the cream, like late afternoon light."
   - Lebih crisp: "Sharper line work, more graphic, slightly higher contrast."

4. **Density**:
   - Minimal: "Even more minimal composition. Only 2 main elements. Almost
     half the canvas is empty cream space."
   - Layered: "Add 2-3 more small supporting ink illustrations as background
     details, like an old explorer's notebook page filled with sketches."

## ✅ Quality Check Pasca-Generate

Sebelum pakai, cek:

- [ ] **Cream background dominan** (#F5F1E8 atau dekat), bukan putih bersih atau abu-abu
- [ ] **Mustard sebagai single accent** — bukan banyak warna ramai
- [ ] **Tidak ada teks/huruf nyasar** — regenerate kalau ada
- [ ] **Aspect ratio benar** — kalau melenceng, regenerate
- [ ] **Tidak ada wajah manusia realistik** (kita pakai abstract silhouettes)
- [ ] **Tidak ada watermark** atau corner artifacts
- [ ] **Mood editorial/refined** — bukan cartoon-y, bukan playful tech
- [ ] **Asymmetric balance** dengan ruang putih jelas (bukan terlalu padat)

Kalau lebih dari 2 check gagal, regenerate lebih hemat daripada edit di Photoshop.
