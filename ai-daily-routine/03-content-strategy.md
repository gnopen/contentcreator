## Positioning

**AI Daily Routine** is counter-positioned against hype AI content:
- Slow over viral
- Editorial over flashy
- Premium over accessible
- One operator's actual stack over "10 best tools" listicles

Audience expectation set in week 1: this is a **book unfolding chapter by chapter**, not a content firehose.

## Algorithm Rules

### TikTok
- 3-second hook is everything — bold visual + large text overlay
- 21-34 second video = 2026 sweet spot
- Reply to top comments with video (free reach boost)
- Never upload other-platform watermarks
- Use TikTok-native trending audio

### Instagram Reels
- Upload original (no TikTok watermark — IG suppresses)
- IG-native trending audio (not TikTok audio)
- Short caption + 3-5 hashtags (not 30)
- Stories daily = active-signal to algo

### Hook Bank (5 Formulas)

1. POV: kamu pakai AI agent buat [task]…
2. Saya nggak bisa kerja tanpa [tool/skill] ini.
3. Stop pakai ChatGPT kayak gini, coba ini…
4. [Task] 2 jam jadi 5 menit, begini caranya…
5. Ini yang saya harap saya tahu waktu mulai pakai AI.

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| KPI miss by Day 25 | Delay class launch; extend pre-launch 1-2 wks |
| Burnout on daily posting | Batch produce 7 chapters/week on Sundays |
| Algorithm change mid-series | Keep producing — quality content survives changes |
| Audience saturation on AI topic | Diversify with niche series (QA, creator, founder spins) |
| Class doesn't fill | Continue free content; relaunch with different angle |
