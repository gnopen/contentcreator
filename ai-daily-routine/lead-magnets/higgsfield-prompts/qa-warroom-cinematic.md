## Tips for Best Results

1. **Run 3 generations** — pick the one with the most realistic faces and convincing UI elements; AI video still struggles with hands and small text
2. **Re-prompt the UI** — if dashboards look fake, generate that scene separately with prompt focused only on UI design, then composite
3. **Add ambient audio** — overlay subtle keyboard/server room sound + cinematic synth pad in post (e.g., Artlist, Epidemic Sound)
4. **End with brand lock-up** — last 2 seconds: your logo/handle on the dark wall, lower thirds reveal "@yourhandle"
5. **Aspect ratio** — render in 16:9 for landing pages, also re-render in 9:16 for vertical (TikTok/Reels)
